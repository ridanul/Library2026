ABOUT THE LIBRARY

A Gateway to Knowledge

Kishoreganj University Central Library is an academic resource center dedicated to supporting the University's teaching, learning and research activities.

The library provides access to books, journals, electronic resources and other scholarly materials for students, faculty members, researchers and staff of Kishoreganj University.

Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where knowledge can be discovered, shared and transformed into meaningful academic achievement.
